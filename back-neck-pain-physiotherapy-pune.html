A thorough, individual assessment

Back and neck pain can have many contributing factors. We discuss your symptoms, health history, work and activity demands, and what you want to get back to. An appropriate physical assessment helps guide the next steps.

Practical movement and exercise

Depending on the assessment, your plan may include advice, mobility exercises, progressive strengthening, movement retraining and strategies to manage activity. The aim is to help you build confidence and capacity, not to chase one perfect posture.

When to seek urgent medical care

Physiotherapy is not a substitute for urgent medical assessment. New or worsening weakness, numbness around the groin or saddle area, loss of bladder or bowel control, severe pain after significant trauma, fever with back pain, or other sudden concerning symptoms need urgent medical attention.

Common questions

Is posture the only cause of back or neck pain?

No. Symptoms can be influenced by several factors, including activity, strength, mobility, workload, recovery and health history. Assessment helps determine what is relevant in your case.

Should I stop all activity?

Not usually, but the right level of activity depends on your symptoms and health situation. We can discuss suitable modifications after assessment.
