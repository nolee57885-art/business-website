Assessment and recovery planning

Sports injuries vary widely. Your assessment considers how the injury happened, current symptoms, training history, movement, strength and the demands of your chosen activity. The plan is adapted to the injury and your stage of recovery.

Build capacity progressively

Rehabilitation may include mobility, strength, balance, coordination, conditioning and sport-specific drills. Loading is progressed according to your response and relevant clinical guidance, rather than relying on a one-size-fits-all exercise list.

Preparing to return to sport

Returning to sport is a process. We work towards the physical demands that matter to you, review progress and discuss a sensible progression back to training and participation. Return-to-sport decisions depend on the individual and may require input from your wider healthcare team.

Common questions

Can I come in with a recent injury?

Yes, you can enquire about an assessment. If you have severe pain, significant swelling, deformity, inability to bear weight or other concerning symptoms, seek prompt medical assessment.

Do you help with gym and running injuries?

Yes. The assessment and rehabilitation plan can be tailored to gym training, running and other sporting or recreational activities.
