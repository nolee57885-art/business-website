Understanding your symptoms

Musculoskeletal pain can involve joints, muscles, tendons and other structures. Symptoms are also influenced by activity levels, sleep, stress, previous injuries and the demands of everyday life. An assessment helps us understand your history and what movements or activities are difficult for you.

A plan built around you

Depending on your needs, your physiotherapy plan may include movement and strength assessment, education, mobility work, exercise therapy, movement retraining and progressive loading. Hands-on techniques may be considered where appropriate, alongside active rehabilitation.

What we may work towards

Your goals may include improving confidence with daily movement, building strength and capacity, managing activity-related symptoms, or returning to work, exercise and hobbies. Progress is reviewed and the plan adjusted as you go.

Common questions

Do I need a scan before an appointment?

Not necessarily. Bring any existing reports or scans, but an initial conversation and assessment can help determine whether further medical review or investigations may be appropriate.

Can I continue exercising?

That depends on your symptoms and assessment. We can discuss suitable activity modifications and a gradual approach to returning to exercise.
