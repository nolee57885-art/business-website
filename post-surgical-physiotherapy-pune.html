Start with your surgical guidance

Recovery needs differ depending on the procedure, tissues involved, health history and your surgeon’s protocol. Please bring discharge notes, operation details and any movement or weight-bearing restrictions provided by your surgical team.

Progress at the right stage

Where appropriate, rehabilitation may include supported movement, range-of-motion work, strength exercises, walking or functional practice. Exercise selection and progression are guided by healing timelines and your treating team’s advice.

Work towards everyday function

Goals may include greater ease with daily activities, rebuilding strength and confidence, and gradually returning to work, exercise or hobbies. Progress is individual and is reviewed over time.

Care that starts with a conversation

Appointments are by prior booking. The clinic is in Viman Nagar, Pune. Home visits may be available in Viman Nagar, Kalyani Nagar, Mundhwa and Koregaon Park, subject to suitability and availability.

Common questions

When should I start physiotherapy after surgery?

Follow the timeline and precautions given by your surgeon. Contact the clinic with your procedure details and current instructions so we can discuss whether an appointment is appropriate.

What should I bring?

Bring your discharge summary, operation notes if available, medication list and any rehabilitation protocol or restrictions from your surgeon.
